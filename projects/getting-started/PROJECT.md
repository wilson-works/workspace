Welcome to WorkSpace from WilsonWorks. This course takes you from opening your office for the first time to building an agent for your own work.

Each lesson is short and hands-on. You paste prompts into a Claude Code chat, watch what happens in your office, and tick off a checklist at the end. Every prompt sits in a box with a **Copy** button. Where a prompt has something in angle brackets, like `<your code folder>`, replace it with your own answer before you send it.

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| GS-01 | Find yourself on the office floor and send your first note |
| GS-02 | Give the office your name, colors and logo |
| GS-03 | Write a CLAUDE.md so Claude knows who you are |
| GS-04 | Set up permissions so Claude can work without you watching |
| GS-05 | Reach your office from your phone and other computers (optional) |
| GS-06 | Answer questions from sessions and use the group chat |
| GS-07 | Meet the CTO org and give it its first job |
| GS-08 | Make the org fit your work |
| GS-09 | Build a specialist agent for a job you repeat |

Go in order. Each lesson builds on the ones before it. GS-05 is the only one you can skip.

When you finish a lesson, tap **Mark done** at the top of its page and the ring above fills in.
