A course for bookkeepers, tax preparers and the people who run the office. You will use AI mostly for accounting questions and admin help, so this course starts there: asking good questions, getting sources, running your week from boards and your calendar, handing work to Tony, and, at the end, building an agent of your own for a job you repeat.

Each lesson is short and hands-on. You paste prompts into a Claude Code chat opened on your **Hub** folder, watch what happens in your office, and tick off a checklist at the end. Every prompt sits in a box with a **Copy** button. Where a prompt has something in angle brackets, like `<your question>`, replace it with your own words before you send it.

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| FS-01 | Find your way around your office and your Hub |
| FS-02 | Ask a good question and get a good answer |
| FS-03 | Research with Louise, with a source for every fact |
| FS-04 | Run your work from boards and your calendar |
| FS-05 | Hand work to Tony, and know where a person signs off |
| FS-06 | Meet the CTO org and give it a job that isn't software |
| FS-07 | Build your own agent with Louise and the CTO org |

Go in order. The [staff manual](../../guides/staff/manual.md), the [FAQ](../../guides/staff/faq.md), [best practices](../../guides/staff/best-practices.md) and the [firm prompt library](../../prompts/firm.md) are there whenever you need them.

Keep client details out of practice work: the lessons use an invented company, Harbor Lane Coffee, and client codes like C01.

When you finish a lesson, tap **Mark done** at the top of its page and the ring above fills in.
