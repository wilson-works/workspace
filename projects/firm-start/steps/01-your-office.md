Your office is a page in your browser that shows your work: your day, your tasks, your calendar, your agents, and every Claude chat running on your computer. Your Hub is the one folder where all of that work lives. In this lesson you find your way around both.

A word you'll see a lot: a **chat** (or session) is one Claude Code conversation. Always open it on your **Hub** folder.

## What you'll get

- The office open and bookmarked.
- Your first chat on your Hub, on the **Floor**.
- A feel for the six pages: Today, Floor, Work, Calendar, Agents and Universe.

## Do this

**1. Open the office.** Go to http://127.0.0.1:4316/ and bookmark it. If the morning box asks to set up your day, press **Later** for now.

**2. Open a chat on your Hub.** In VS Code: File, Open Folder, then `Hub` in your home folder. In the Claude app: the Code tab, then `Hub`. Start a chat and paste:

```
I'm new here. In plain words, tell me what is in my Hub: each top folder and what it is for. Don't change anything.
```

**3. Find yourself on the Floor.** Open **Floor** in the office. Your chat is at a desk, with a line saying what it is doing.

**4. Walk the pages.** Click each tab: **Today** (your day), **Work** (your boards and this course), **Calendar** (your week), **Agents** (your assistant, Tony, Louise and the CBFC Academy door) and **Universe** (who did what).

**5. Meet the fence.** Paste:

```
Read the file Documents/test.txt in my home folder.
```

## What you should see

- The office in CBFC's look, with your chat on the Floor.
- A plain list of your Hub's folders: `00-Inbox`, `10-Business`, `50-AI` and the rest.
- In step 5, Claude says `Outside the Hub: ...` and reads nothing. That is the Hub fence keeping the rest of your computer private.

## If something's off

- **The office page doesn't open.** Paste the install line from guide 11, step 5, again: it starts the office and changes nothing else.
- **Claude read a file outside the Hub.** Check the chat is open on the `Hub` folder itself, not your home folder. Tell the office manager if it still reads outside.

## Done when

- [ ] The office is bookmarked and you have opened each of its six pages.
- [ ] You have a chat open on your Hub and know what each top folder is for.
- [ ] You have seen the fence refuse a file outside the Hub.
