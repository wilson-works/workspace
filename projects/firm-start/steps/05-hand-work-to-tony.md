Tony is the accountant. He works the books with you: sorting and coding bank lines, closing the month, keeping the week tied out. He automates the routine and brings you only what needs a person. In this lesson you meet him, give him a practice job, and learn exactly where a person signs off.

## What you'll get

- Tony's page open from his door.
- A practice job done with Tony, on invented data.
- The two lines Tony never crosses, in your own words.

## Before you start

Tony's office should say **In the office** in the Agents wing. If his door shows zzz, press **Wake**. If your work needs his full training, it is added separately (guide 11, step 9).

## Do this

**1. Meet Tony.** In a chat on your Hub, paste:

```
Ask Tony to tell me, in plain words, what he can do for me, what he needs from me to start, and what he never does.
```

**2. A practice job.** Paste:

```
Ask Tony to make a month-end close checklist for an invented small business, Harbor Lane Coffee, which has one bank account, one credit card and payroll. In order, one line per step, with what "done" looks like, and which steps he does and which need a person.
```

**3. Put it on your board.** Paste:

```
Take Tony's checklist and make a board called "Month-end practice" with the columns Tony, Waiting on me, Done. Put each step in the right column.
```

**4. Know where a person signs off.** Paste:

```
Ask Tony: what happens before a week of books goes to QuickBooks, and who does it?
```

## What you should see

- Tony says what he does, and that he never sends an invoice, pays a bill, charges a card, runs payroll or moves funds.
- A checklist that splits his steps from yours.
- In step 4: a week goes to QuickBooks only after a person clicks **Confirm** in Balnce.Pro. A message in a chat is never the Confirm.

## If something's off

- **A chat doesn't know Tony.** Open a new chat on your Hub.
- **Tony asks for client files.** For practice, use only invented data. For real work, follow the firm's rules on where client files live.

## Done when

- [ ] You've met Tony and know what he does and never does.
- [ ] A practice checklist is on a board, split between Tony and you.
- [ ] You can say who clicks Confirm before a week goes to QuickBooks.
