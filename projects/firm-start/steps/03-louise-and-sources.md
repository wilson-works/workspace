Louise is the research librarian. You give her a question; she finds the answer with a source for every fact, marks anything she could not verify, and keeps what she found on shelves you can browse. For accounting and tax questions, she is where to start.

## What you'll get

- Your first research run with Louise.
- A book on her shelves you can come back to.
- The habit of checking a source before you rely on it.

## Before you start

Louise's office should say **In the office** in the Agents wing. If her door shows zzz, press **Wake**.

## Do this

**1. Ask Louise a real question.** Pick one you need answered for your work, with no client details. In a chat on your Hub, paste:

```
Louise, research <your accounting or tax question>. Give me a source for every fact, say how current each source is, and mark anything you could not verify as "not verified". End with a short answer in plain words.
```

**2. Watch her work.** Open her door in the **Agents** wing (**Step inside**). Her page shows each stage of the run. A full run can take a while: carry on with other work and come back.

**3. Read it like a reviewer.** When she has shelved it, open the book on her page. Pick two facts and open their sources. Do they say what she says? Are they current?

**4. Find it again.** In a new chat, paste:

```
Louise, what do we have on <the topic>?
```

## What you should see

- Louise at work on her page, then a new book on her shelves.
- Every fact with a source, and anything unsupported marked "not verified".
- The same book found again from a new chat.

## If something's off

- **A chat says it doesn't know Louise.** Open a new chat on your Hub: a chat reads its agents when it starts.
- **A source doesn't say what she said.** Tell her: "The source for <fact> doesn't support it. Check it again." Then trust that fact less until it is fixed.
- **Her run is taking a long time.** That is normal for a full run. Come back later.

## Done when

- [ ] Louise researched a real question and shelved a book.
- [ ] You opened at least two of her sources and checked them.
- [ ] You found the book again from a new chat.
