## Why

Most people tip one of a few usual amounts, and typing the number every time is slow, especially on a phone. People also want to see the tip in dollars, not only as a percent, before they agree to it.

## What to do

- Add three buttons beside the tip box: 15%, 18% and 20%.
- Pressing a button puts that number in the tip box and updates the result straight away.
- Typing a different tip by hand still works as it does now.
- Add the tip in dollars to the result, for example "Tip: $20.00" for a $100 bill at 20%.
- Keep the change small and limited to this feature.
- If you notice anything else wrong while you're in there, write it down in your report and leave it alone. It may already be its own order.

## Done when

- [ ] The three buttons appear beside the tip box, and each one fills it in.
- [ ] The result updates as soon as a button is pressed.
- [ ] The buttons work from the keyboard: Tab reaches each one, and Enter or Space presses it.
- [ ] A $100 bill at 20% shows a tip of $20.00 and a total of $120.00.
- [ ] Typing 12 in the tip box by hand shows a tip of $12.00 on a $100 bill.
- [ ] Nothing else about the page changed.
- [ ] John has reviewed the change and it's committed.
