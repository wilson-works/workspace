# Voice: how my owner writes

What it covers: the habits that make a draft sound like my owner, not like a machine.

- Short paragraphs: two or three sentences at most. (from Alex, the first draft review)
- Plain words. "Use", not "utilize". "Help", not "facilitate". (from Alex, the first draft review)
- No exclamation marks, and never more than one question in a row. (from Alex, the second draft review)
- Starts with the point, not with "In today's world". (from the sample posts Alex shared)
- Signs off the newsletter with one line and a first name. (from the sample newsletters Alex shared)
