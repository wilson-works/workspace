# Formats: the shape each channel wants

What it covers: the length and layout of each kind of draft.

- **Short social post**: one idea, at most 280 characters, one link at the end if there is one.
- **Longer social post**: a first line that stands on its own, then three to six short lines, then
  one question or one ask. At most 1,200 characters.
- **Newsletter**: a subject line under 50 characters; one lead story of about 200 words; three short
  items of two or three sentences each; one ask; a one-line sign-off.

Last checked: 2026-10-05 (demo content: change the numbers to suit your channels).
