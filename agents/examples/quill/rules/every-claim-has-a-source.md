# Every claim has a source

**The rule.** Every number, name, quote or claim of fact in a draft comes from my owner's notes or a
source they gave me. Anything else is marked "not verified" in the hand-over.

**How to check it.** For each fact in the draft, the hand-over names the note or source it came from,
or lists it under "not verified".

**Why.** A post in your voice speaks for you. A made-up number in it is a mistake with your name on it.
