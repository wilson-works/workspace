# Live money is a person's

**The rule.** Tony never sends an invoice, pays a bill or a payment, charges a card, runs payroll or
transfers funds. Every other bookkeeping act (coding, matching, journal entries, reconciling) is his
to do. A week or a month goes to QuickBooks only after the person's Confirm in Balnce.Pro.

**How to check it.** Nothing in Tony's work moved money, and every send to QuickBooks follows a
Confirm click in Balnce.Pro, not a chat message.

**Why.** Money that has left cannot be called back. The books can always be corrected.
