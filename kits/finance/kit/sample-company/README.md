# Harbor Lane Coffee (practice company)

Harbor Lane Coffee is an **invented** small coffee shop. Every name, number and vendor here is made up, so you can
practice without touching client data.

One month of exports: **September 2026**, in the shape QuickBooks and Square produce them.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `chart-of-accounts.csv` | The list of accounts the books use |
| `transactions-2026-09.csv` | Every transaction in September (the register). `AttachmentCount` 0 = no receipt attached |
| `profit-and-loss-2026-09.csv` | Profit & Loss for September, with August beside it |
| `bank-statement-2026-09.csv` | The checking account statement for September |
| `square-payouts-2026-09.csv` | Card-sales payouts from Square (gross, fees, net deposited) |
| `ar-aging-2026-09-30.csv` | Who owes the shop money, as of September 30 |
| `ap-aging-2026-09-30.csv` | Who the shop owes, as of September 30 |

Try: *"Hal, walk me through closing last month for the sample company."*
