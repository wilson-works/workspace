# 00-Inbox

**What goes here:** downloads, scans and anything you have not sorted yet. It is a queue, not a home.

**What never goes here:** anything you mean to keep here. Every file leaves for its zone home within a week.

**Example:** `invoice-2026-10-02.pdf` lands here from Downloads, then moves to `10-Business/GreenThumb/Invoices/`.

Empty it every week. When you do not know where something belongs, it may wait here until then, and no longer.
